# Digest Product Video Design

## Style Prompt

Dark premium AI education SaaS product reveal. The film should feel like a restrained keynote opener: deep navy and ink-black space, glass UI, soft cyan glow, violet graph accents, precise Chinese typography, and smooth product motion. The story is emotional but not melodramatic: a student's scattered learning materials become a persistent course knowledge system. Avoid presentation-slide logic; use product memory, cinematic sequencing, and UI transformation.

## Colors

- Background: `#050B14`, `#08111F`
- Surface: `#0F1A2B`, `#142238`, `#182C46`
- Text: `#F4F8FF`, `#A9B8CD`, `#6D7E96`
- Accent cyan: `#20E3B2`
- Accent blue: `#3BA7FF`
- Accent violet: `#7C5CFF`
- Warning contrast: `#FF5A7A`

## Typography

- Primary Chinese sans: `Microsoft YaHei`, `PingFang SC`, `Arial`, `sans-serif`
- Technical mono: `Consolas`, `Courier New`, `monospace`
- Display treatment: 900 weight headlines, 300-400 body, compact letter spacing only on labels.

## Motion

- Smooth, confident, product-like. Use blur crossfades and soft push transitions via scene overlap.
- Cards fly in with shallow 3D rotation, then settle.
- Materials are absorbed into a product workbench through cyan light paths.
- Knowledge nodes appear in waves, then connect with glowing lines.
- Camera motion is subtle: slow push-in, parallax, scale shifts; no jumpy edits.

## What NOT to Do

- No cartoon characters, no robot mascot, no stock-photo montage.
- No dense PPT sections, budgets, team roles, or timelines.
- No excessive gradient text or neon overload.
- No large paragraphs; one core sentence per screen.
- No visible "screenshot placeholder" labels in the final frame; replacement slots are marked in HTML attributes only.
