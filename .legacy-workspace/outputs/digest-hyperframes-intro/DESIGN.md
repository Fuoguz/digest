## Style Prompt

Calm futuristic academic tech for a university innovation-project defense, project showcase, and business-plan presentation. The film should feel like a quiet research interface becoming legible: deep blue-black space, restrained violet-blue atmosphere, fine node lines, soft particles, precise typography, readable Chinese subtitles, and product-demo moments that look like a real web app. The mood is serious, credible, academic, and intelligent rather than flashy or game-like.

## Colors

- Background: `#050912` near-black with a cool blue cast
- Surface: `#101827` academic interface panels
- Surface Soft: `#172235` elevated panels and product frames
- Primary Text: `#F4F8FF` large readable Chinese copy
- Secondary Text: `#AEBBD0` explanatory copy and labels
- Cyan Accent: `#69E7F2` nodes, labels, signal lines
- Violet Accent: `#8F7BFF` soft glows and AI emphasis
- Warm Paper: `#F2F5F8` desaturated document/cards used sparingly

## Typography

- Main Chinese UI and subtitles: system `sans-serif` so HyperFrames can render consistently without missing-font warnings
- Technical labels and numbers: system `monospace`
- Large Chinese subtitles must stay at 56px or above; product title and closing logo may be larger.

## Motion

- Scene transitions use calm blur crossfades or focus-pull style dissolves.
- Entrances are staggered, soft, and directional; no hard jump cuts.
- Ambient motion stays subtle: drifting nodes, slow glows, small particle movement, gentle product frame parallax.
- Flow section moves left to right with a measured academic explainer rhythm.
- New 85-second structure: pain point, limitation of summaries, Digest positioning, four-step product demo, scenarios, business path, value summary, closing.
- Voiceover is calm and concise; captions stay readable long enough for projection.

## What NOT to Do

- No game-poster neon, explosions, or aggressive glitch.
- No tiny captions; defense-room readability matters.
- No overloaded identical card grid; keep each module legible and purposeful.
- No pure black/white defaults; keep neutrals tinted toward the palette.
- No busy stock-style imagery; use Digest interface screenshots as the product signal.
- No exaggerated revenue claims; business model language should read as a verifiable future path.
